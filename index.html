What We Do

Serving neighbors across San Diego County.

Volunteers receive food from the San Diego Food Bank, sort it into meal boxes, and distribute it through a drive-through pickup designed to be practical, welcoming, and respectful.

Prepared Food Boxes

Food boxes are prepared ahead of distribution days so the team can serve households with care, efficiency, and flexibility.

Drive-through Distributions

Distribution days are set up as drive-through pickups, making it easier for families and individuals to receive support in a convenient way they can count on week after week.

Volunteer-Packed Meal Boxes

Volunteers arrange a practical mix of proteins, vegetables, fruit, and canned goods into food boxes that help support individuals and families.

About Feeding the Flock

Rooted in compassion, built around community.

What began as a simple calling to feed the hungry has grown into a hands-on community effort built around organized food distributions, volunteer service, and practical care for neighbors across San Diego County.

Rooted in Christian compassion, Feeding the Flock SD also supports ministry work in Mexico alongside its local food programs. Learn more about our story, our outreach, and the communities we serve.

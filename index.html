What We Do

Serving neighbors across San Diego County and beyond.

Feeding the Flock SD supports neighbors through practical food programs shaped by dignity, flexibility, and care, from drive-through distributions and senior pantry support to emergency food assistance, student support, homeless outreach, and cross-border service.

Drive-through Distributions

Families can receive food through convenient drive-through distributions where volunteers load boxes directly into cars. The goal is simple: practical help offered with dignity, respect, and kindness.

Senior Pantry

The Santee Senior Pantry gives older adults a welcoming way to choose groceries that fit their needs, with volunteer help and delivery support for housebound seniors when available.

Emergency Pantry

The emergency pantry at the La Mesa Veterans' Club helps neighbors facing urgent food needs between regular distributions, offering a local point of care when timing matters.

Schools, Outreach, and Mexico

Feeding the Flock SD also supports a local school partnership, homeless outreach, and missionary and charity work in Mexico, extending care beyond a single program or location.

About Feeding the Flock SD

Rooted in compassion, built around community.

What began as a simple calling to feed the hungry has grown into a hands-on community effort built around food distributions, senior support, emergency assistance, student food support, homeless outreach, and practical care for neighbors across San Diego County.

Centered around dignity, love, and compassion, Feeding the Flock SD uses food as a bridge to relationship, trust, and support, while also serving through faith-based ministry work in Mexico.

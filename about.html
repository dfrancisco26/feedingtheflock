Serving our community with food, compassion, and dignity.

Our Story

Born from a simple calling to feed people and care well for them.

Feeding the Flock SD was founded with a simple mission: to feed the hungry, honor the dignity of every person served, and show up for neighbors in times of need. Today, that mission takes shape through organized food distributions, volunteer service, outreach, and ministry support.

Our work is shaped by Christian compassion, but our service is open-hearted and practical. Volunteers help receive food from the San Diego Food Bank, sort it into food boxes with items like proteins, vegetables, fruit, and canned goods, and serve those boxes through a welcoming drive-through pickup.

Our Mission

Feeding the Flock SD exists to relieve hunger, restore dignity, and care for people through organized food distributions, compassionate outreach, and ministry support.

  • Serve people with dignity, compassion, and respect
  • Provide practical food support where it is needed most
  • Strengthen community through faith-rooted service
Programs

Programs built around practical help and compassionate service.

Feeding the Flock SD serves through practical, volunteer-led efforts that help food reach people in a way that is organized, accessible, and respectful.

Prepared Food Boxes

Volunteers sort and pack food into boxes ahead of distribution, helping each pickup day run smoothly and respectfully.

  • Built around practical food staples and fresh items when available
  • May include proteins, vegetables, fruit, and canned goods
  • Prepared with care for families and individuals in need

Drive-Through Food Distributions

Distribution days are set up as drive-through pickups, making it easier for people to receive food support in a convenient and dignified way.

  • Designed to be organized, accessible, and welcoming
  • Helps households receive prepared food boxes efficiently
  • Creates a steady rhythm of local support

Community Outreach

Beyond distribution days, Feeding the Flock SD looks for ways to meet practical needs and extend care to people in the community.

  • Supports neighbors facing food insecurity or unstable circumstances
  • Extends care through presence, dignity, and consistency
  • Leaves room for the work to grow as needs become clearer

Ministry Support in Mexico

Feeding the Flock SD also supports Protestant ministry work in Mexico as part of its broader commitment to service and Christian compassion.

  • Reflects the organization’s faith-rooted service mission
  • Extends support beyond local county lines
  • Connects local generosity with cross-border outreach

How Donations Are Used

Your support fuels our mission.

Donations help support the day-to-day work behind Feeding the Flock SD's food programs, including drive-through distributions, pantry support, outreach supplies, school partnerships, and practical care for neighbors in need.

Every gift helps strengthen a volunteer-driven effort focused on serving people with consistency, compassion, dignity, and flexibility.

Your generosity allows us to continue our programs and offer them to more families and individuals.

  • Weekly drive-through food distribution events in Jamul and La Mesa
  • The Santee Senior Pantry and delivery support for housebound seniors
  • Emergency food assistance at the La Mesa Veterans' Club
  • School partnerships to provide food for students after school and on weekends
  • Supplies and support for ministry work across the border
  • Homeless outreach that values dignity, compassion, and understanding
  • ... and so much more.

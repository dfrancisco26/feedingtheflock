How Donations Are Used

Where your support goes.

Donations help support the day-to-day work behind Feeding the Flock SD’s food distributions, including the supplies, logistics, outreach, and practical care that help each distribution come together.

Every gift helps strengthen a volunteer-driven effort focused on serving people with consistency, compassion, and dignity.

What donations help support

  • Food box distributions for families and individuals in need
  • Supplies used for sorting, packing, and serving food
  • Outreach efforts and practical support in the community
  • Ministry support connected to Feeding the Flock SD’s work in Mexico

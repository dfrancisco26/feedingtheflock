Feeding the Flock SD — Website Editor Guide
===========================================

This is a friendly, step-by-step guide to updating the website yourself — no
coding, and nothing you can permanently break. If something looks confusing,
you can always close the page without saving and nothing changes.

**The dashboard lives at:** <https://feedingtheflocksd.org/admin/>

> First time here? You'll need a free GitHub account and an invite from the site
> owner — that's a one-time setup covered in
> [`SETUP-CMS.md`](SETUP-CMS.md#for-the-editor-first-time-github-setup). Come
> back once you can log in.

<a name="how-it-works"></a>

## 1. How it works (the big picture)

- You make changes in simple forms and click **Save**.
- Each save is published automatically. The live website updates itself,
  usually **within a minute or two**.
- There's no separate "database" and nothing to back up — your words and photos
  *are* the website.
- Every change is recorded with your name and can be undone by the site owner,
  so it's safe to experiment.

You don't need to understand anything more technical than that.

---

<a name="logging-in"></a>

## 2. Logging in

1. Go to <https://feedingtheflocksd.org/admin/>.
2. Click **Login with GitHub**.
3. The very first time, GitHub asks you to **Authorize** the app — click the
   green button. You won't be asked again after that.
4. You'll land on the dashboard, with a list of sections down the left side.

To sign out, use the account menu (your name/avatar) in the top corner.

---

<a name="getting-around"></a>

## 3. Getting around the dashboard

Down the **left side** are the sections you can edit:

| Section | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Events / Calendar** | Everything that shows on the events calendar |
| **Photos** | The photo gallery |
| **Impact Numbers** | The three big numbers on the homepage |
| **Site Details** | Address, email, social links, donations, contacts |
| **About Page** | Programs, locations, and partner logos |

Click a section to open it. Some sections hold a list of items (like events or
photos); click an item to edit it, or use the **Add** button to make a new one.

The right side is where you type. When you've made a change, look for the
**Save** button (usually in the top-right).

---

<a name="saving-and-publishing"></a>

## 4. Saving and publishing your changes

1. Make your edits in the form.
2. Click **Save** (top-right).
3. That's it — your change is published. Give the site **1–2 minutes**, then
   refresh the public page to see it live.

If you leave a page **without** clicking Save, nothing is changed — so it's
always safe to look around.

<a name="events"></a>

## 5. Events / Calendar

This controls what appears on the events calendar.

### Add an event

1. Click **Events / Calendar** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Event**.
3. First, choose **how the event repeats**:
   - **Repeats every week** — for something that happens the same day every week
     (e.g. a Saturday pantry).
   - **Repeats monthly** — for something like "the 4th Wednesday of every month."
   - **On specific date(s)** — for one-off or a handful of specific days.
4. Fill in the form (the fields change depending on what you picked):
   - **Event name** — e.g. "Senior Pantry."
   - **Day of the week** — a dropdown (Sunday through Saturday). *(Weekly and
     monthly events.)*
   - **Which occurrence each month** — First / Second / Third / Fourth / Fifth.
     *(Monthly events only — e.g. "Fourth" + "Wednesday" = 4th Wednesday.)*
   - **Date(s)** — click to pick dates on a calendar; use **Add** for more than
     one. *(Specific-date events only.)*
   - **Time** — free text, e.g. "8:30 AM" or "4:00–6:00 PM" or "Time TBD."
   - **Short description** — optional, one sentence.
5. Click **Save**.

### Edit or remove an event

- **Edit:** click the event in the list, change what you need, click **Save**.
- **Remove:** open the event list, and use the item's menu (the "⋯" or trash
  icon) to delete it, then **Save**.

<a name="photos"></a>

## 6. Photos

This is the gallery of photos on the website.

### Add a photo

1. Click **Photos** in the sidebar, then open **Gallery**.
2. Click **Add Photo**.
3. Under **Photo**, click to **upload a new image** from your computer (or pick
   one that's already been uploaded).
4. Under **Describe this photo**, write a short sentence about what's in the
   picture — for example, "Volunteers packing food boxes on a distribution day."
   (This is read aloud to people using screen readers and helps the photo show
   up in searches, so please don't skip it.)
5. Click **Save**.

### Edit, reorder, or remove photos

- **Change a description:** click the photo in the list, edit the text, **Save**.
- **Reorder:** drag photos up or down — the gallery shows them in that order.
  See [Reordering](#reordering).
- **Remove:** use the photo's menu (⋯ or trash icon) to delete it, then **Save**.

> **Photo tips:** landscape (wide) photos usually look best. Very large files
> from a phone are fine, but if you have the option, a photo around 2000 pixels
> wide loads faster.

<a name="impact-numbers"></a>

## 7. Impact Numbers

These are the three big numbers on the homepage (pounds of food, people served,
volunteer hours).

1. Click **Impact Numbers** → **Homepage Impact Numbers**.
2. You can edit the small label, heading, and subheading at the top.
3. Under the numbers, each one has:
   - **The number** — digits only, no commas (type `79595`, not `79,595`; the
     website adds the commas for you).
   - **Symbol after the number** — optional, like `+` (used for "15,400+").
   - **What it counts** — the sentence under the number.
4. Click **Save**.

Keep it to three numbers — that's what the homepage layout is designed for.

<a name="site-details"></a>

## 8. Site Details

Organization-wide information. You won't need this often.

1. Click **Site Details** → **Site Details**.
2. Here you can update:
   - **Organization name, tagline, EIN, nonprofit disclosure**
   - **Logo** and **contact email**
   - **Mailing address** and **social links** (Facebook, Instagram)
   - **Food box sign-up form URL**
   - **Donation → Payment methods** — PayPal, Venmo, Zelle: their links, logos,
     QR code images, and whether each one shows on the site (the **Show on site**
     switch).
   - **Contacts / board** — names, roles, and emails.
3. Click **Save**.

> **One caution:** each payment method has a small **"ID (do not change)"**
> field. It quietly connects the button to its QR-code pop-up. Leave it as-is
> unless you're adding a brand-new payment method.

<a name="about-page"></a>

## 9. About Page (programs, locations, partners)

Everything under the **About Page** section.

- **Programs** and **Locations** — each has a **Title**, a **Description**, and a
  list of **Bullet points**. Use **Add** to add a program/location or a bullet
  point; drag to reorder; use the item menu to remove.
- **Partners** — each has a **Name** and a **Logo image**. Add a partner by
  uploading its logo and typing its name.

Click **Save** when done.

---

<a name="reordering"></a>

## 10. Reordering things (drag and drop)

Anywhere you see a list — photos, programs, locations, partners, bullet points —
you can change the order by **dragging** an item up or down using its handle
(the dotted or ☰ grip on the left of each item). The website displays things in
the order you arrange them here. Remember to click **Save**.

You don't manage any "order numbers" — the order you see is the order visitors
see.

---

<a name="tips"></a>

## 11. Tips for great content

- **Keep descriptions short and warm** — a sentence or two is plenty.
- **Always describe photos** in the "Describe this photo" box (accessibility).
- **Check dates and times** before saving events.
- **Preview on your phone** — after saving, open the live site on your phone to
  see how it looks to most visitors.
- **One change at a time** is easier to double-check than ten at once.

<a name="troubleshooting"></a>

## 12. If something goes wrong

**"My change isn't showing on the website."**
Give it 1–2 minutes and refresh. If it's still not there, make sure you clicked
**Save**. Your browser may also be showing an old copy — try a hard refresh
(hold **Shift** and click reload).

**"It won't let me log in."**
Make sure you accepted the GitHub invite from the site owner, and that you're
clicking **Login with GitHub**. If it still fails, contact the owner — your
access may need to be re-sent.

**"I think I made a mistake / deleted something."**
Don't worry. Every change is saved in history, and the site owner can restore
any previous version. Just tell them roughly what and when.

**"I can't find where to edit X."**
Check the section table in [Getting around](#getting-around). If a piece of text
isn't in any form, it may be part of the page design — ask the site owner.

**Still stuck?** Contact the site owner/administrator — they can see the full
history and fix anything.
